Class description

Grades: 7th - 12th

Aspiring music producers will turn ideas into original tracks while exploring the fundamentals of beatmaking and digital music production in Ableton. Students will create beats, shape sounds, and experiment with sampling, arranging, mixing, and mastering as they discover how music is built from the ground up. Along the way, producers will explore the science of sound, collaborate with peers, and develop their own unique style in a creative, hands-on studio environment.

Resources

Ableton has an extensive catalog of resources about music theory and production techniques. Some of the most applicable for this class are given below.

Music theory

These free, browser-based resources are the best places to start learning about music theory in a production context:

  • Learning Music (Ableton): interactive lessons on beats, notes, scales, chords, basslines, and song structure. The closest match to what we do in class, and a great place to review each week.
  • Learning Synths (Ableton): a hands-on introduction to how synthesizers work: oscillators, filters, envelopes, and LFOs.
  • Making Music (Ableton): short, practical essays on creative problems, like getting unstuck and finishing tracks.

Videos

The official Ableton YouTube channel has many helpful videos on the software and on music-making in general. Two playlists fit this class especially well:

  • Start Learning Ableton Live: an 8-part series of short videos in which a producer walks through the basics of beatmaking in Live.
  • Live Tips: clips under 90 seconds each, meant to spark ideas or quickly show off a useful feature.

Keyboard shortcuts

The biggest skill in electronic beatmaking is capturing the sounds in your head quickly and getting them into the software. The key to working fast is knowing the keyboard shortcuts. Here is the complete list of Ableton keyboard shortcuts. Start with these:

Cmd/Ctrl + T
Insert audio track
Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + T
Insert MIDI track
Cmd/Ctrl + D
Duplicate
Tab
Switch between Session and Arrangement View
M
Turn your computer keyboard into a piano
B
Draw mode

Curriculum

Each week lists what we covered, key terms, the song we listened to, and something to try at home. New weeks are added as the class goes.

Week 1: Welcome to Ableton

What we covered
Introductions, a tour of Ableton Live, and the difference between MIDI and audio. We programmed a basic drum pattern and wrote notes in the piano roll, then closed with a listening exercise to break a song down into its parts.
Key terms
DAW, MIDI, audio, clip, track, piano roll, BPM
Listening
[NOBLE - F3miii]
Try at home
Keep listening to music! Check out BandLab, a free DAW you can use on your phone.

Week 2: Sorry I missed you!

Began working through the Learning Music module created by Ableton
Key terms

Try at home
